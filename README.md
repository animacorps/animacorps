### `00 / OVERVIEW`

> DOCUMENT CLASSIFICATION: INTERNAL  
> AUTHORIZATION: LEVEL 01  
> STATUS: OPERATIONAL

The regiment operates at the intersection of military discipline and unexplained phenomena. Its personnel investigate regional folklore, contain anomalous threats, and preserve knowledge of entities that exist beyond conventional understanding.

**Mission parameters**
- Investigate reports of folkloric activity.
- Secure and document anomalous locations.
- Maintain operational discipline and chain of command.
- Preserve restricted records and recovered artifacts.
